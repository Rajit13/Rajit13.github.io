IOAA 2025 context
The green task nodes are derived from the official 2025 observation question papers: OM (Sky Map), OT (Telescope), and OP (Planetarium). The paper is first decomposed into its scored tasks and the skill implied by each task; those task documents are then placed in the same text/skill vector space as the Star Charts 101 full-text corpus. The resulting lines are therefore evidence-based preparation associations, not claims that a particular exam question came from the book and not predicted scores.

How we made this

From full book text to the cluster

The TOC is used only for navigation and page labels. The semantic graph is built from the complete current TeX source: each section/subsection body is cleaned, long bodies are divided into text windows, and every window enters the same corpus as the recognized IOAA 2025 tasks.

1 · Full-text representation

For term \(q\) in document \(d\), the page uses log-scaled TF–IDF:

\[x_{q,d}=\left(1+\ln f_{q,d}\right)\left[\ln\!\left(\frac{N+1}{n_q+1}\right)+1\right].\]

Here \(f_{q,d}\) is the term count, \(n_q\) is the number of corpus documents containing the term, and \(N\) is the total number of book windows + topic seeds + exam-task documents.

2 · Association strength

Book topics and exam tasks are compared with cosine similarity:

\[s_{ij}=\frac{\mathbf{x}_i\!\cdot\!\mathbf{x}_j}{\|\mathbf{x}_i\|\,\|\mathbf{x}_j\|}.\]

Each IOAA task keeps its three strongest book-topic matches. For a task worth \(m_j\) marks, its graph attraction is \(w_{ij}=s_{ij}\sqrt{m_j}\). Marks affect visual importance, not semantic similarity.

3 · Topic importance

A book topic becomes visually larger when it is both central inside the book and relevant across the observation tasks:

\[R_i=\frac{\sum_j m_j s_{ij}}{\sum_j m_j},\qquad I_i=0.70\frac{R_i}{\max R}+0.30\frac{k_i^{(w)}}{\max k^{(w)}}.\]

\(k_i^{(w)}\) is weighted graph degree. This emphasizes concepts that bridge several tasks rather than one repeated keyword.

4 · Force-directed layout

The cluster is ForceAtlas2/LinLog-inspired. All nodes repel, while connected nodes attract with their association weight:

\[F^{\rm rep}_{ij}=k_r\frac{(1+I_i)(1+I_j)}{d_{ij}^2+\epsilon},\qquad F^{\rm att}_{ij}=k_a w_{ij}\ln\!\left(1+\frac{d_{ij}}{d_0}\right).\]

The \(x\)- and \(y\)-positions have no astronomical meaning. Neighbourhood, edge strength, and cluster membership are what should be read.
